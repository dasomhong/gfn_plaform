FinGeo — Global Financial Network Platform

Research prototype · Multi-year platform · Default reference year 2022

Data Sources & Reuse

This research platform combines publicly available statistical datasets published by the OECD, the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the Bank for International Settlements (BIS), together with Natural Earth map geography. The analytical views, network construction and interpretation are produced for this prototype and are not endorsed by the source organisations.

Scope and transformations

  • The default reference year is 2022; available years vary by source (see table below). the BIS LBS layers use fourth-quarter positions. Data retrieval dates and exact source-release vintages are not identifiable from the supplied HTML and should be added when available.
  • The financial network layers use harmonised economy codes, source-specific directions, country aggregations and transformations into millions of US dollars where necessary.
  • The country–industry input–output analyses include direct intermediate-service and final-use relationships. Any displayed direct value-added allocation uses a uniform source-industry value added / output coefficient and is not a full indirect input–output multiplier analysis.
  • IMF investment and BIS banking data are outstanding positions (stocks), not observed transaction flows. Missing and undisclosed positions should not be interpreted as zero.
  • Comparisons across data sources must account for different country coverage, survey definitions, and directions of observation. Source-specific validation notes are also included within the interactive preview.
