Relationship Definitions & Interpretation

Production links describe production or value-added relationships. Financial links generally describe reported positions or stocks at a point in time. A change in a position is not a transaction flow: valuation, exchange rates, reporting changes and coverage can also contribute.

Missing observations remain missing. Geographic totals, concentration measures and exploratory groups describe the observed network and depend on coverage. Compare like-for-like sources and read each relationship’s direction.

Indirect value-added relationships are not presented until a validated calculation is available.
